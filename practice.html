📋 Practice 1 Cheat Sheet & Algorithm Comparison

Tokenization Unit 1

Tokenizer Arena

word_tokenize handles natural text; wordpunct_tokenize isolates punctuation; TreebankWordTokenizer handles standard Penn Treebank contractions.

Stemming Unit 2

Stemming Slicers

Rule-based heuristic cuts (PorterStemmer, SnowballStemmer, RegexpStemmer). Blazing fast for indexing, but may produce non-dictionary stems.

Noise Filter Unit 3

Stopwords Reduction

Filters ~40-50% non-informative words (the, is, in), cutting model dimensionality while preserving core topical signals.

Lemmatizer Unit 4

POS-Aware Roots

Dictionary lookups with WordNet. Passing pos='v' (verb) turns running ➔ run, while pos='a' turns better ➔ good.

Vocabulary Unit 5

FreqDist & Stats

Computes token frequencies and Lexical Diversity (Unique Vocab / Total Tokens). The foundation for BoW and TF-IDF vectors.

Application Unit 6

Review Cleaner

Practical production pipeline: cleans unstructured reviews with emojis and slang into clean topic features ready for sentiment classification.
