📖 Core Functions Breakdown (From Notebook)

Step 1: Sentence Tokenizer sent_tokenize()

Paragraph ➔ Documents (Sentences)

Slices an unstructured paragraph container into individual standalone sentence strings using pretrained sentence boundary detection models.

from nltk.tokenize import sent_tokenize
documents = sent_tokenize(corpus)
for sentence in documents:
    print(sentence)
Step 2: Word Tokenizer word_tokenize()

Document ➔ Words (Standard English Rules)

Standard NLTK word boundary tokenizer. Correctly treats common English contractions by separating the negation or possession suffix: isn't ➔ ['is', "n't"], it's ➔ ['it', "'s"].

from nltk.tokenize import word_tokenize
for sentence in documents:
    print(word_tokenize(sentence))
Step 3: Punctuation Separator wordpunct_tokenize()

Separates Every Quote & Symbol Individually

Uses regex \w+|[^\w\s]+ to separate words from all punctuation characters. Notice: it's is split into 3 distinct tokens: ['it', "'", 's']!

from nltk.tokenize import wordpunct_tokenize
for sentence in documents:
    print(wordpunct_tokenize(sentence))
Step 4: Penn Treebank Standard TreebankWordTokenizer()

Preserves Periods on Abbreviations & Standard Treebank Rules

Conforms to the classic Penn Treebank tokenization standard. It treats words with fullstops as whole tokens (e.g. Python. or U.S.A.) and normalizes quotes.

from nltk.tokenize import TreebankWordTokenizer
tokenizer = TreebankWordTokenizer()
tokenizer.tokenize(corpus)
