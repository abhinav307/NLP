The complete original paragraph containing all text.

▼ Step 1: Split Paragraph into Sentences
📄 Stage 2: Document
Document = Sentence 0 Documents

Each individual sentence inside the paragraph is treated as one Document.

▼ Step 2: Tokenize (Split Sentences into Individual Words)
🏷️ Stage 3: Word
Word = Token 0 Tokens

Sentences are sliced into individual words. In NLP, every word is an indexed Token.

▼ Step 3: Remove Repeated Words & Deduplicate
🔤 Stage 4: Vocabulary
Vocabulary = Unique Word 0 Unique Words

Repeated tokens are removed! Only unique distinct words remain in the Vocabulary set.

Quick Visual Summary: The 4 Sub-topics

1. Corpus Corpus = Paragraph

Rule: The entire text paragraph is 1 Corpus.

"Cats like fish. Dogs like fish. Cats and dogs both love fish!"
👉 1 Paragraph container
2. Document Document = Sentence

Rule: Each separate sentence is 1 Document.

Doc 1 "Cats like fish."
Doc 2 "Dogs like fish."
Doc 3 "Cats and dogs both love fish!"
👉 3 Sentences = 3 Documents
3. Word Word = Token

Rule: Every individual word is counted as a Token.

#0Cats #1like #2fish #3Dogs #4like #5fish #6...
👉 Counted as Tokens (includes duplicates)
4. Vocabulary Vocabulary = Unique Word

Rule: Duplicates removed! Only unique words are kept.

and 1x both 1x cats 2x dogs 2x fish 3x like 2x love 1x
👉 Counted as Unique Vocabulary Set
