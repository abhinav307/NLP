📖 Why Stopwords Removal is Critical in NLP

Noise Reduction Signal-to-Noise

40–50% Data Compression

Words like the, is, in, to, a account for roughly half of all word counts in English prose. Removing them significantly cuts training time and memory footprint.

Corpus Lexicon 179 Stopwords

Curated NLTK Corpus

NLTK includes 179 curated English stopwords in nltk.corpus.stopwords. Always lowercase tokens before checking membership against the stopword set!

Machine Learning BoW & TF-IDF

Prevents Skewed Features

If stopwords aren't removed, basic frequency models will treat the as the most important word in every document. Removing them allows real keywords to dominate!
